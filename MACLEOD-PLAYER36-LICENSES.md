## Integration contract and resource bounds

- BOMusicData.tracks retains 19 master entries: the previous 17 plus these two. Existing original score definitions are unchanged.
- BOMusicData.tracksForPlayerLevel(actual) and BOAudio.tracksForPlayerLevel(actual) expose 17 entries below actual level 36 and 15 at/above 36. New entries have unlockPlayerLevel:36. casino_rooftop, poker_rain, poker_smoke and baccarat_moon have retirePlayerLevel:36; their scores are not deleted.
- BOAudio.configure({playerLevel: actualInteger, track: id, ...}) enforces that catalog. Missing initial/invalid actual level fails closed to level 1. Later omitted level keeps the previously supplied actual level. config.musicLevel is not the player level and is ignored by this gate. Level 1..100 integers only; no numeric-string coercion. Locked, retired and unknown track IDs normalize transient audio to poker_lounge; no profile or saved preference is written by audio.
- BOMusicData.licensed remains the legacy Deadly Roulette descriptor. BOMusicData.licensedTracks is the three verified recording descriptors including the two additions. Assets are local data URLs or exactly the selected same-origin filename; no third-party runtime streaming, redirect, credentials or file:// fetch fallback is permitted.
- status().assetStatus/error describe the SELECTED ID, not another track's decode. Additional diagnostic fields: assetTrackId, assetCacheTrackId, assetCacheBytes, assetLoadTrackId, playerLevel, and assetStates[id] with status/error. States: idle, queued, loading, decoding, ready, failed. Original fallback playback is never labeled licensedPlaying.
- Compressed MP3: maximum 12 MiB per file; embedded URL: maximum 18 MiB; decoded: duration 1..300 seconds, 1..2 channels, maximum 30,000,000 samples / 120,000,000 retained Float32 bytes. The longer 268-second George recording requires the new bounded 300-second duration cap.
- ONE realtime decoded cache entry, evicted before loading a different recording. ONE actual fetch/native decode lease shared with explicit previews. 20-second consumer timeout aborts fetch where possible, reports failure, and never autoplays late results. Native decodeAudioData cannot be canceled: its lease remains occupied until actual settlement, preventing repeated timeouts/track swaps from spawning unlimited parallel native decodes. A permanently hung native decode needs a reload to free that lease; background fallback remains honest, never a retry storm.
- A single explicit licensed renderOffline preview may own its separate input buffer until rendering settles; it never populates the realtime cache or changes realtime preferences/transport. New-track previews require overrides.playerLevel:36. Concurrent previews/busy decode reject asset_decode_busy instead of silently fabricating a licensed preview. Memory accounting is retained PCM, not a claim that total browser/process peak memory is only 120 MB: graphs, media strings/compressed bytes, offline input, native internals and short fading voices also use memory.
- Before fetching/decoding, known metadata duration × target AudioContext sample rate × two channels must also fit the sample budget; a high-rate context such as 96 kHz fails honestly rather than allocating a known oversized George buffer. Native decoder internals are still browser-owned, and post-decode validation remains necessary.
- BOAudio.moneyGain(bank) is a separate original three-note chime. The caller must confirm an actual positive money award and pass the post-gain Bank; audio does not read wallet/state or invent an award. Finite negative debt/zero Bank share the lowest pitch. Positive represented Bank values use a smooth logarithmic pitch shift below 18 semitones, with finite-number validation, a 100-ms coalescing gate, original voice limits and existing trusted unlock/mute/hidden/sound-volume safety. The existing coin/chip/XP effects and their paths are unchanged; nonfinite, nonnumeric values are silent. No claim of arbitrary-precision wallet arithmetic is made by this sound helper.
- music-assets.js grows to 31,977,368 bytes. The two new base64 payloads total 25,177,444 bytes, in addition to the unchanged Deadly Roulette payload. Load both music-assets.js and music-data.js before audio.js. Parent owns app/level picker/credits/build/website/service-worker integration.
